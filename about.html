About me

I'm Cash Gines, a CRM administrator in training who likes taking a process everyone works around and turning it into one people can rely on.

For the past eight years I've worked where data, systems and people meet. At Tuacahn Center for the Arts I built the reporting infrastructure from the ground up, ran the website and presented to the Board of Directors each quarter. Since 2022 I've been at Southwest Technical College, where I build program and course schedules in the student information system, create dashboards, manage end-of-course surveys and pull together accreditation documentation.

I'm a lifelong learner. I recently finished the Software Development program at STECH and I'm now in the CRM Administration program at Dixie Tech. Outside of work you'll find me in the mountains, taking on side projects or planning the next trip.
